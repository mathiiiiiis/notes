# Privacy Policy for Notes

**Last updated:** 30 July 2026

## Summary

Notes does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you
write stays on your device. The app has no internet permission, so it is not
technically capable of sending your notes anywhere.

## What the app stores

| Data              | Where it is stored                                 | Why                                       |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Note text         | Local SQLite database in the app's private storage | To show you your notes                    |
| Images you attach | The app's private file storage                     | To display them inside a note             |
| Timestamps        | Alongside each note                                | To order the list by most recently edited |

That is the complete list. There are no accounts, no analytics, no crash
reporting SDK, no advertising identifiers, and no third party services.

## Permissions

Notes requests **no** Android permissions.

Attaching an image uses the system photo picker. The picker returns only the
single image you choose, and the app copies it into its own private storage. The
app never receives access to your wider photo library, which is why no storage or
photo permission is needed.

## Legal basis for processing

No personal data is processed by the developer, so no legal basis under GDPR
Art. 6 is engaged. Data you create is stored locally on your own device and
remains under your control at all times.

## Device backup

If you have Android Backup enabled in your system settings, your notes and
attached images may be included in the backup Google makes of your device. That
backup is handled by Google under
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), not by this app.
You can disable it in Android Settings, under System, Backup.

## Your rights

Because no data leaves your device, there is nothing held by the developer to
request access to, correct, export, or erase. You can:

- Delete an individual note in the app
- Delete all app data via Android Settings, Apps, Notes, Storage, Clear storage
- Uninstall the app, which removes its private storage including all notes and
  attached images

## Children

Notes contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no user-generated content sharing,
and no data collection. It is suitable for all ages.
